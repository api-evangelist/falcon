# Falcon FS Documentation

> Falcon is to issuance what Stripe has been to acquire. Falcon’s mission is to co-create the most innovative financial products of the future. We are solving a problem that we ourselves faced in our early days while launching our commercial payments vertical. Falcon is a global embedded finance platform going after a $24B+ market, equipped with its own lending and issuance licenses. It brings the speed and flexibility that these tech companies need & enables them to launch in days and not months, with an almost zero upfront cost. Falcon offers a unique combination of developer-first APIs & no-code solutions built on top of a highly flexible & scalable modular architecture.
